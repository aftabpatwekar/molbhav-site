Get the app

Get Molbhav on your phone

Free on Android and iPhone — no app store needed.

Android
Download APK

Android 5.0+ · ~61 MB · full voice support

  1. Tap Download APK.
  2. Open the file; allow “Install from this source.”
  3. Open Molbhav and sign up.
iPhone & Web
Open the web app

Any browser · nothing to install

  1. Open the link in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome.
  2. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
  3. Launch it like a normal app.

Tip: voice entry works on Android and desktop browsers; on iPhone the web app uses fast typed entry (Apple doesn’t allow web voice input).
