Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Molbhav app and website (the “Service”), operated by Aftab Patwekar (“we”, “us”, “our”), collect, use, and protect your information. We keep it simple: we only collect what the app needs to work for you.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address, and — if you sign in with Google or Apple — the basic profile identifiers those providers return.
  • Profile details you provide: display name, and optionally a profile photo, date of birth, gender, phone number, and address.
  • Financial data you enter: transactions (amounts, categories, notes, dates), accounts, budgets, tags, recurring rules, and receipt images you choose to attach.
  • Voice input: speech you dictate is converted to text on your device by your operating system; we store the resulting text, not the audio.
  • Group data: if you create or join a group, your group membership and any transactions you share into that group.
  • Contact messages: if you write to us through the website form, we receive the name, email, and message you submit.

2. How we use your information

  • to provide and operate the Service and your account;
  • to store and sync your data across your devices;
  • to generate the charts, budgets, and summaries you ask for;
  • to enable shared-group features you opt into;
  • to send you notifications you enable (such as daily reminders);
  • to respond to your support or contact requests.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your financial data for advertising.

3. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored using Supabase, our database and authentication provider. Access is protected by row-level security so that, by default, only you can read your own records — except content you explicitly share into a group, which becomes visible to that group’s members. Receipt images are kept in a private storage bucket accessible only to you.

4. Third-party services

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and file storage.
  • Google / Apple — only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • Web3Forms — delivers messages submitted through the website contact form to our email.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy terms.

5. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. Deleted transactions are held briefly (soft-deleted) so you can undo, then removed. When you delete your account, we delete your associated personal data, subject to any backups that age out on a rolling basis.

6. Your rights

You can view and edit your profile and transactions directly in the app, export your data (CSV, Excel, or PDF), and request deletion of your account and data. To exercise any privacy right, email aftab.patwekar@smarte.pro.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures — encrypted connections, authentication tokens, and per-user access rules — to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to safeguard your information and take backups.

8. Children’s privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International users

Your data may be processed on servers located outside your country. By using the Service, you consent to such processing in line with this policy.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, an in-app notice.

11. Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email aftab.patwekar@smarte.pro.
